One extra step for historical imagery

Sorry about the extra step. To play older pictures of Earth and save them as a video, you’ll need to add a small helper to your browser. It’s called the Viśvadṛś History extension. Your browser needs this helper to get the older pictures from Japan’s NICT image archive.

I’ve chosen this approach to help protect your privacy and keep the viewer free. Pictures come straight from NICT to your device, and your video is made right here in your browser. Neither passes through a server I run. You don’t need an account or another app running in the background. If this isn’t your computer, ask its owner before installing anything.

Chrome

  1. Download the Chrome extension. Open the downloaded ZIP file to get a folder. Keep that folder somewhere you can find it; don’t delete it while you’re using the extension.
  2. In a new Chrome tab, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, then click Load unpacked.
  4. Choose the folder you just opened from the ZIP file. The extension should be named Viśvadṛś History. Allow it to get images from NICT if your browser asks.
  5. Come back to this page and press the browser’s reload button. Choose History, pick a date and start and end times, then click Load. These times are in UTC, the shared world time, so they may differ from your local clock.

Firefox and Zen

The Firefox and Zen download isn’t ready yet. When it is, you’ll open the download in your browser, choose Add and allow access to NICT. Then come back here, reload the page and choose History.

You only need to do this setup once. You can still see the latest pictures without the helper. This helper doesn’t work in Safari.
